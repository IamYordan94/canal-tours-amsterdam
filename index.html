Why book with us

Why book your Amsterdam canal cruise?

Book an unforgettable canal cruise through Amsterdam and experience the magic of the city from a whole new perspective. Glide under centuries-old stone bridges through the heart of the historic city centre, past charming houseboats and rows of 17th-century houses that line the UNESCO World Heritage-listed canals.

Our Best of Amsterdam Canal Cruise departs every hour from seven central mooring points: Rijksmuseum, Central Station, Damrak and many more. This cruise is ideal for couples looking for a romantic moment, for families exploring together or for solo travellers who want to get to know Amsterdam in a unique way. With panoramic views, fresh air and interesting commentary, it's one of the most beautiful and relaxing ways to explore the city.

  • ✓ Panoramic views & fresh air
  • ✓ Interesting live commentary
  • ✓ Free cancellation
  • ✓ Departs every 30 minutes
