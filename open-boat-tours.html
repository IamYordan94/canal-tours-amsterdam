01

See Amsterdam the way it was meant to be seen

Effortlessly floating through its breathtaking canals. Forget crowded streets and packed tourist boats — our intimate open boat cruise lets you soak in the fresh air and stunning views in total comfort. Depending on availability the cruise will be one of several boat sizes — from 12 persons up to 40 persons per sloop.

02

Uncover city secrets

Explore hidden and narrower canals that the bigger canal cruises can't access. Your skipper serves as your personal guide and will show you anything you'd like to see, just ask! Our skippers are treasure troves of historical secrets about Amsterdam and will gladly tell you everything you need to know.

03

Boat tours on a smaller sloop

Looking for something a bit more intimate? Our canal boat tours Amsterdam on small, open electric boats are perfect for those who prefer to see Amsterdam unobstructed. These quiet, eco-friendly boats glide effortlessly through both hidden corners and popular routes of the canal belt.
