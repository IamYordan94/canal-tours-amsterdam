Discover Amsterdam's charm

There's no better way to explore the Dutch capital than by water. Our Amsterdam canal tours offer a relaxing, scenic way to see the city's famous sights. Imagine gliding through the historic canals, listening to stories and fun facts shared by your guide — either through headphones or in person. A peaceful and immersive experience that's perfect for travellers of all ages.

02

Sail past the city's top highlights

From the Skinny Bridge and the Golden Bend to the Anne Frank House and the famous 17th-century canal houses — our Amsterdam canal tours are designed to give you the perfect overview of the city's rich history and charm.

03

Day cruises & magical evenings

Our canal cruises run throughout the day, so you can explore in broad daylight or choose a magical evening cruise. As dusk falls, the water reflects the warm lights of bridges and buildings, creating a calm and romantic atmosphere.
